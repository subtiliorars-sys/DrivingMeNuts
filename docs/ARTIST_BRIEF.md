# Pixel Art External Asset Brief — Driving Me Nuts

## Overview
**Project:** Driving Me Nuts (P1)
**Style Reference:** Stardew Valley, A Short Hike, Dinkum (warm, forgiving, hand-drawn feel in pixels)
**Base Scale:** 32×32 px (NPCs, items, truck parts)
**Viewport:** 480×270 px (16:9)
**Palette Depth:** 16–32 colors per scene
**Style:** Chunky, readable silhouettes; slightly rounded corners. Solid fills without gradients (unless shading is added over flat values).

## Color Palette (Palette A: Golden Market)
For P1, all final pixel art MUST use Palette A (Golden Market):
- **Truck body:** `#8B6F47`
- **Truck trim:** `#F5DEB3`
- **Ground / grass:** `#7CB342`
- **Sky / backdrop:** `#FFFFCC`
- **Market awning:** `#FF9800`
- **Roasted peanut:** `#6B4423`
- **Burlap / raw sack (and mascot base skin tone):** `#D2B48C`
- **Shadow:** `#99663D`
- **Coin / gold:** `#FFD700`
- **Smoke wisp:** `#E8E8E8`
- **NPC skin:** `#E8D4B0`
- **UI Panel background:** `#F5DEB3`
- **UI Panel border:** `#8B6F47`
- **UI Panel text:** `#2C2416`
- **Warning / alert:** `#C0392B` (from ART_BIBLE.md)

## Mascot / Character Direction
**Primary Mascot:** Legsy
- Wide oval pod body, two short thick legs, stubby round arms, oval head directly on body (no neck), large dot eyes, small open-mouth grin. 
- *Crucial restriction: No top-hat + monocle combinations to avoid trademark infringement.*

## Sprite Delivery Specifications
- All multi-frame sheets: **horizontal layout** (frames left-to-right).
- Max sheet size: **1024×1024 px**.
- Provide transparent backgrounds for all sprites (excluding the backdrop).
